This is the living blueprint we share with partners. It explains how the BlackFrame stack snaps together, who each subsystem serves, and how we onboard teams without slowing down their production calendars.

Why codify a pillar?

The blog is our daily pulse, but collaborators needed a canonical overview they could send to producers, narrative leads, or CTOs. AI Engine Foundations is that source of truth. It highlights the decisions that keep our worlds creator-led while still letting autonomous agents handle the grind.

Simulation Spine

Procedural physics and systems simulation tuned for stylized sci-fi worlds. Every module exports deterministic snapshots so QA can diff playtests instead of replaying entire builds.

Narrative Codex

A Markdown-first canon that powers our vibe coding workflow. Specs remain the boss; the engine only ships code when prose, constraints, and safety rails align.

Creator Co-Pilots

Task-focused agents for level design, dialog iteration, and live-ops planning. They sit in editors you already use and report every change back to the shared canon.

Ops & Observability

Telemetry streams that watch agent runs, classify anomalies, and trigger human reviews. Partners get dashboards tuned for ship-room rituals, not generic AI metrics.

How we onboard partners

Studios that book a capabilities call enter a six-step ramp:

  • Discovery pulse: We unpack your pipeline, target platforms, and content cadence.
  • Sandbox alignment: Our engineers stand up a sandbox with your asset samples and custom guardrails.
  • Co-pilot rehearsal: We plug creator agents into your live editor for a scripted sprint.
  • Simulation stress test: Combined team runs battle drills against scope creep and failure modes.
  • Telemetry tuning: Observability feeds route to your ops stack with alerting tuned to your rituals.
  • Launch playbook: Weekly check-ins graduate to a documented live-ops plan.
“Our goal is transferable velocity — your team should feel faster the week we leave than the week we arrive.”
