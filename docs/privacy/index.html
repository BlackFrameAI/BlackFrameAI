Privacy & Transparency

How we protect visitors, partners, and our team

BlackFrame AI publishes intentional transparency about our founder while shielding sensitive details about the rest of the team. This page outlines the personal data we collect, how we use it, and the rights available to visitors in different jurisdictions.

What we collect

  • Manual inquiries: Emails sent to studio@blackframeai.org, press@blackframeai.org, or other @blackframeai.org addresses include the details you provide. We store them in encrypted mailboxes and shared CRM notes.
  • Blog comments: Utterances embeds authenticate through GitHub. Your GitHub username and comment content are public in the associated GitHub issue.
  • Server logs: Cloudflare provides anonymized request metrics (IP truncated, no fingerprinting) so we can detect abuse.
  • Analytics: Plausible analytics captures aggregate page views and events without cookies. It records the page URL, referrer, and approximate region only; IP addresses are discarded and nothing is tied to an individual.

How we use data

  • Respond to partnership, press, and community inquiries.
  • Share verified studio updates when you explicitly request them.
  • Monitor aggregate engagement so we can catch broken journeys without profiling visitors.
  • Monitor service health, abuse, and fraud attempts.

We do not sell or rent personal data. Automated decision making is limited to spam filtering on our email providers.

Legal basis & retention

For EU/UK visitors we process data under legitimate interest (responding to inbound requests) or consent (newsletter opt-ins). We retain messages for up to 24 months unless you request deletion sooner.

Your rights

  • Request access, updates, or deletion by emailing privacy@blackframeai.org.
  • For Utterances comments, edit or delete the GitHub issue directly or email us to expedite moderation.
  • Residents covered by GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or similar regulations can invoke local rights; we will respond within the required timelines.

Security controls

  • HSTS, CSP, and other HTTP headers are enforced through Cloudflare (see the runbook below).
  • Web forms require CSRF double-submit tokens and Cloudflare Turnstile verification before submissions reach the team.
  • Responsible disclosure details live at /.well-known/security.txt.
