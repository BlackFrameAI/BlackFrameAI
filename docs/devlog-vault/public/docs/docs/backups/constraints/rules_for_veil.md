## Satire Guidelines and Risk Mitigation

- **Purpose**: Keep the setting sharp yet respectful, avoiding legal or cultural conflicts.

- **Satirical Themes**:
    - **Eternal Empire**: High-fantasy crusade iconography, zealous rhetoric, and cathedral-scale architecture. Direct references to real-world faiths are omitted in public materials.
    - **Crescent Veil**: Mystical theocratic order that blends crescent motifs, ceremonial law, and veiled silhouettes. Parallels to real-world belief systems are intentionally abstracted.
    - **Skitterkin**: Xenos swarms that symbolize pressure from outside powers without mapping to contemporary groups.

- **Risk Mitigation**:
    - No real-world names, events, or cultural groups in public releases.
    - Satire must remain balanced across factions.
    - Ensure every faction is clearly fictional and grounded in sci-fi worldbuilding.
    - Menu disclaimer: "*Purge of the Crescent Veil* is an original work of fiction."

- **Visuals**:
    - **Eternal Empire**: Aurorite armor, ornate weaponry, grand crusade banners.

    - **Skitterkin**: Original insectoid silhouettes and technologies.

- **Audio**:
    - Layer grimdark choirs with industrial ambience.
    - Narrator options vary by Chapter or role, including:
        - Vox-filtered drill sergeant
        - Inquisitor tone
        - Librarian/scholar tone
        - Specialist perspectives linked to faction choices
    - Utilize fictional chants and vocalizations for cult elements.

## Licensing and IP Avoidance Strategy

- Commercial release planned; ensure unique IP presentation.
- Factions, unit names, and mechanics must remain original.
- Core inspirations (non-exhaustive):
    - Starship Troopers
    - Warhammer 40k (tone and thematic inspiration only)
    - Fallout
    - Gundam
    - Historical crusade aesthetics (generalized and public domain)
    - Gothic sci-fi traditions
    - Roguelike and action titles such as Hades, Brotato, Binding of Isaac, Path of Exile, Vampire Survivors, and Tiny Rogues
- Avoid direct use of protected trademarks, names, or distinctive designs.
- Conduct a final IP review before any public launch.
