# Environmental Chemistry Manifest (Sanitized)

The public version removes operational formulas while keeping a lightweight overview of how atmospheric factors influence materials.

---

## Oxidation and Surface Weathering

- Highlight that moisture, oxygen, and temperature accelerate corrosion without publishing laboratory-rate constants.
- Encourage qualitative staging of rust or patina progression based on artistic direction and safety review.
- Remind readers that any aggressive corrosion modeling must pass the internal compliance checklist.

---

## Airborne Gas Interactions

- Summarize common atmospheric constituents that interact with exposed materials in high-level terms.
- Avoid listing chemical reaction pathways or catalytic triggers that could be repurposed irresponsibly.
- Point teams toward internal analytics dashboards for detailed pollutant timelines instead of external studies.

---

## Biological Decomposition Considerations

- Describe mold and microbial growth as dependent on humidity, temperature, and substrate nutrients.
- Keep implementation guidance qualitative; defer to internal biosafety reviewers for quantitative parameters.
- Include a reminder that visual storytelling should prioritize public health messaging when depicting decay-heavy scenes.
