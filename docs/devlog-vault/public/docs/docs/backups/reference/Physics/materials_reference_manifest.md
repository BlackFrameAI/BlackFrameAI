# Materials Reference Manifest (Sanitized)

The following categories point teams toward broadly accessible knowledge sources for material behavior while omitting proprietary citations.

---

## Aerospace and Advanced Materials

- Consult publicly available aerospace engineering textbooks or open courseware when modeling high-heat or high-abrasion surfaces.
- Capture qualitative takeaways such as "maintains strength under thermal cycling" or "requires layered insulation" instead of copying raw tables.

---

## Protective Systems and Ballistics

- Use open academic studies on impact mitigation to inspire armor tuning. Summarize findings in internal docs with relative scales (light, medium, heavy protection).
- When referencing historical standards, replace explicit designations with generalized language so external readers cannot trace back to restricted specifications.

---

## Trauma and Wound Modeling

- Draw from peer-reviewed medical journals or public health resources that discuss injury patterns in broad terms.
- Focus on translating observations into gameplay cues—blood pooling rates, stagger severity, tissue damage states—without embedding classified measurements.
