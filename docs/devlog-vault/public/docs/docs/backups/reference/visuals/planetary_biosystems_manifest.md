# Planetary Biosystems Manifest — Visual Toolkit

This public-facing toolkit summarizes the planetary biosystem references used for Codex art direction. High-resolution surveys, commercial texture libraries, and proprietary GIS layers are kept offline.

---

## Crust Signatures & Mineral Storytelling
- **Source Category:** Educational geology exhibits and open geological map interpretations.
- **Shared Data:** Color palettes for ore belts, fracture silhouettes, and erosion motifs.
- **Withheld Data:** Precise claim boundaries, satellite raster downloads, and industrial partner identifiers.

---

## Climate Staging & Weather Visuals
- **Source Category:** Public climate explainers, documentary footage, and creative commons weather libraries.
- **Shared Data:** Jet stream-inspired skies, precipitation mood boards, and storm lifecycle beats.
- **Withheld Data:** Broadcast restriction notes, location metadata, and proprietary compositing templates.

---

## Exotic Atmospheres & Chemistry
- **Source Category:** Popular science astrobiology features and open educational resources.
- **Shared Data:** Color grading guides, particle behavior themes, and hazard signage inspiration.
- **Withheld Data:** Laboratory spectroscopy outputs, mission telemetry, and custom shader code snippets.

---

## Usage Guidance
- Provide this summary to external artists building planetary vistas or hazard signage.
- Escalate to the archive maintainers before incorporating restricted survey material or licensed textures.
