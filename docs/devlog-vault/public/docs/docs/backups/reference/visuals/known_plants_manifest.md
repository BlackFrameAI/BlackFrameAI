# Known Plants Manifest (Public Summary)

This digest outlines the botanical knowledge channels referenced by Codex while omitting agency names, subscription-only archives, and internal map overlays.

---

## Botanical Identification & Medicinal Use
- **Source Category:** Aggregated horticultural registries and open herbal compendiums reviewed for public release.
- **Data Provided:** Species naming conventions, growth habits, native range summaries, and non-prescriptive remedy notes.
- **Design Role:** Seeds foraging encounters, cultivation quests, and biome ingredient tables without exposing protected datasets.

---

## Toxic & Psychoactive Flora
- **Source Category:** Sanitized toxicology overviews assembled from declassified ethnobotanical field notes.
- **Data Provided:** Compound families, qualitative potency tiers, and general safety advisories.
- **Design Role:** Enables poison crafting, altered-perception events, and encounter balancing using generic risk language only.

---

## Geometric Patterns in Flora
- **Source Category:** Public academic discussions on phyllotaxis, spiral growth, and leaf arrangement geometry.
- **Data Provided:** High-level pattern descriptors (spiral, whorl, rosette), scaling trends, and narrative symbolism cues.
- **Design Role:** Drives procedural foliage generation, relic ornamentation, and botanical sigil work without citing proprietary diagrams.

---

- Share this summary with worldbuilding teams and contributors who require thematic alignment only.
