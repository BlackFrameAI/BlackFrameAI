Interaction Guidelines (Sanitized)

The development workflow relies on structured collaboration between human operators and AI assistants. The following habits ke
 ep the process reliable without referencing individual practices:

- Request clarifications whenever requirements feel ambiguous.
- Reinforce constraints and acceptance criteria at every major step.
- Document reasoning so future contributors understand why choices were made.
- Accept that mistakes may occur and treat them as signals for iterative refinement.
- Maintain a supervisor mindset: AI output is reviewed, not blindly trusted.
- Separate statements of fact from their supporting evidence before acting on them.

These principles describe the interaction discipline at a high level while avoiding personal identifiers or confidential proces
