# Magnetics and Field Behavior Manifest (Sanitized)

This manifest highlights approachable guidelines for electromagnetic gameplay without referencing restricted datasets or vendor-specific tooling.

---

## Magnetic Fields and Interactions

- Represent magnetic fields with visualizations that make polarity clear; emphasize lines of force or particle effects instead of citing laboratory values.
- Use configurable strength curves so designers can tune attraction, repulsion, or locking behaviors without relying on proprietary calibration constants.
- Encourage iteration based on publicly available demonstrations (e.g., classroom experiments) when artists need inspiration for particle motion.

---

## Electromagnetic Radiation and Shielding

- Describe shielding behavior in qualitative terms: absorption, reflection, or rerouting of energy. Provide sliders or curves to govern efficiency rather than embedding classified specifications.
- When simulating interference, expose noise intensity, drop-out duration, and recovery timing as gameplay parameters that can be iterated safely.
- Cite broadly available educational texts for team members who require additional background on induction, Faraday-style cages, or attenuation.

---

## Advanced Magnetic Manipulation

- For levitation or transit systems, blend lift forces with stabilization logic that can be tuned visually; avoid referencing proprietary vehicle blueprints.
- Keep pulse or burst effects parameter-driven (duration, radius, decay) so EMP-style encounters remain creative without disclosing sensitive engineering data.
- Document any entropy or magic-inspired twists in design docs rather than embedding them as immutable constants in code or content tables.
