# Anthropology Manifest (Sanitized)

This manifest distills publicly shareable guidance for modeling human social evolution, belief systems, and material culture while omitting any direct references to identifiable researchers, proprietary coursework, or gated archives. Apply these notes to guide faction logic, language spread, relic evolution, and behavioral AI stances.

---

## 🏕️ Tribal Societies & Tool Use

- Study open-access museum summaries on early toolmaking, fire control, and migratory patterns to inform pre-technology factions.
- Highlight cooperative scavenging, seasonal camps, and resource scarcity to justify survival-focused AI goals.
- Track cultural drift through artifacts, ritual objects, and shared myth to differentiate clans.

---

## 🗣️ Linguistic Drift & Oral History

- Use freely available language family trees to map dialect splits, trade pidgins, and sacred cant formations.
- Support translation puzzles by defining cognate patterns, honorific rules, and mnemonic storytelling structures.
- Tie oral traditions to quest hints or cultural unlocks so linguistics deepen gameplay rather than remain lore-only.

---

## 🔥 Ritual, Burial, and Kinship Logic

- Reference public anthropology primers for burial customs, ancestor worship, and kin obligation systems.
- Let rites influence faction diplomacy: shared mourning rites might unlock alliances, while taboo violations trigger conflict.
- Build kinship matrices that inform inheritance, leadership succession, and social penalties for breaking lineage norms.

---

## 🧭 Implementation Notes

- Keep ethnographic inspiration generalized; avoid duplicating real-world communities or sacred knowledge verbatim.
- Document any invented rituals or kin diagrams so narrative, gameplay, and AI teams stay aligned.
- Regularly review content with cultural sensitivity checks to maintain respectful representation.
