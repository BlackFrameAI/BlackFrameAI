# Natural Physics Reference Manifest

This manifest highlights resources on environmental physics, including terrain deformation, wind behavior, structural motion, and landscape-driven responses. The collection supports realistic worldbuilding and environmentally aware simulations.

---

##  Wind Dynamics & Aerodynamic Force

**Name:** *Fluid Flow Over Terrain — NOAA Wind Resource Atlas*
**Type:** Government Data Portal
**URL:** https://www.nrel.gov/grid/wind-resource.html

**Use:** Models wind drag over varied landscapes and structures. Informs weather systems, outdoor level design, and renewable-energy research.

---

**Name:** *Aerodynamic Drag & Lift — NASA Glenn Research Center*
**Type:** Aerospace Physics
**URL:** https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/drageq.html

**Use:** Explains how objects move or resist wind forces. Useful for tuning flight models, cloth simulation, and projectile behavior.

---

##  Rockfall, Erosion & Collapse

**Name:** *Rockfall Hazard Simulation — USGS Landslide Program*
**Type:** Geophysical Model
**URL:** https://www.usgs.gov/programs/landslide-hazards/science/rock-fall

**Use:** Details rockslide triggers, fall angles, and mass transport. Supports hazard visualization and dynamic terrain events.

---

**Name:** *Soil Erosion Mechanics — USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service*
**Type:** Agricultural & Earth Surface Physics
**URL:** https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/data-and-reports/water-erosion

**Use:** Describes how terrain changes over time through erosion. Guides environmental storytelling and resource management simulations.

---

##  Vegetation Response & Sway

**Name:** *Tree Movement in Wind — Journal of Experimental Botany*
**Type:** Botanical Physics
**URL:** https://academic.oup.com/jxb/article/60/8/2379/472706

**Use:** Examines how different tree types sway under stress. Helps animate foliage and evaluate structural loading.

---

**Name:** *Grass and Canopy Motion — Real-Time GPU Wind Simulation*
**Type:** Game Engine Rendering Tech
**URL:** https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-ii-real-time-rendering/chapter-6-animated-grass-wind

**Use:** Demonstrates techniques for simulating grass and canopy motion. Useful for creating responsive outdoor environments and visual effects.
