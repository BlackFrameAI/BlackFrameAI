# Language Structure Reference Manifest

This manifest gathers trusted resources for studying spoken and written language systems. The references assist with phonetics research, linguistic worldbuilding, and clear communication design.

---

##  Phonetics & Phonology

**Name:** *IPA Chart (International Phonetic Alphabet)*
**Type:** Linguistic Reference Chart
**URL:** https://www.internationalphoneticalphabet.org/ipa-charts/ipa-symbols-chart-complete/

**Use:** Provides standardized symbols for every phoneme. Supports accurate pronunciation guides and naming frameworks.

---

**Name:** *Articulatory Phonetics — UCLA Phonetics Archive*
**Type:** Audio Dataset & Diagrams
**URL:** http://archive.phonetics.ucla.edu/

**Use:** Includes voice samples and diagrams of speech production. Useful for training performers, tuning synthesis, or analyzing dialogue delivery.

---

##  Language Families & Drift

**Name:** *Ethnologue Language Tree*
**Type:** Global Language Classification
**URL:** https://www.ethnologue.com/

**Use:** Outlines the hierarchy of known human languages and dialects. Helps track linguistic relationships for worldbuilding and localization.

---

**Name:** *Glottolog — Comprehensive Language Catalog*
**Type:** Linguistic Database
**URL:** https://glottolog.org/

**Use:** Documents language families, extinct dialects, and syntactic patterns. Assists with comparative linguistics and research accuracy.

---

##  Constructed Language Systems

**Name:** *How to Create a Language — MIT OCW Linguistics*
**Type:** University Lecture
**URL:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/linguistics-and-philosophy/24-900-introduction-to-linguistics-fall-2012/

**Use:** Covers core principles for building constructed languages, alphabets, and syntax. Helpful for narrative design and creative linguistics.

---

**Name:** *Phonotactics — Wikipedia (Structure Constraints)*
**Type:** Open Reference
**URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonotactics

**Use:** Defines legal sound combinations within languages. Guides the creation of believable syllables and accent rules.
