# Fluid Dynamics Reference Manifest

This manifest supports Codex in simulating liquid and gas flow, including blood trails, water surface interaction, air resistance, and environmental currents. All data is grounded in fluid mechanics.

---

##  Liquid Simulation & Viscosity

**Name:** *Fluid Mechanics  Fundamentals and Applications (Çengel & Cimbala)*  
**Type:** Engineering Textbook  
**URL:** https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/fluid-mechanics-fundamentals-applications-cengel-cimbala/M9781259696534.html  

**Use:** Core reference for laminar vs turbulent flow, flow rate calculations, and viscosity thresholds. Codex can simulate blood pooling, lava creep, or sewage currents.

---

**Name:** *SPH Fluid Simulation  Real-Time 3D Approaches*  
**Type:** Graphics Research  
**URL:** https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/sca03.pdf  

**Use:** Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) system for procedural flow simulation. Codex can use this to render real-time splatter, ocean foam, or viscous enemy remains.

##  Atmospheric & Gas Dynamics

**Name:** *NOAA Fluid Flow Visualizations  Earth Systems Science*  
**Type:** Government Dataset  
**URL:** https://www.esrl.noaa.gov/gsd/fab/airquality/  

**Use:** Large-scale air flow, vortex modeling, pressure transitions. Codex can simulate sandstorms, entropy clouds, or air resistance drag on fast NPCs.

---

**Name:** *Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) for Airflow Simulation*  
**Type:** Engineering Study  
**URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/computational-fluid-dynamics  

**Use:** Solves fluid interactions over complex terrain. Codex may apply this to cave airflow, atmospheric vent zones, or stealth movement detection via air currents.

##  Surface Tension, Splatter, and Breaks

**Name:** *Drop Impact Physics  American Physical Society APS*  
**Type:** Research Article  
**URL:** https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.014501  

**Use:** Describes how drops shatter or merge based on tension and angle. Codex can model blood impact decals, rain pooling, or acidic splash propagation.
