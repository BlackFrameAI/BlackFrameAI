# Plant Biology Manifest

Codex references these biological sources to model realistic plant geometry, growth behaviors, leaf dynamics, vascular structures, and environmental response curves. Used for terrain foliage, herbal systems, and overgrowth logic.

---

## 🌱 Vascular Structure & Water Transport

**Name:** Xylem and Phloem Systems in Plants\
**Type:** University Biology Lecture\
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/scitable/knowledge/library/plant-vascular-tissues-xylem-and-phloem-14301949/

Use: Guides internal flow logic, collapse visuals, and sap bleed mechanics. Supports damage modeling and nutrient simulation.
