# Global Music Styles Manifest

This manifest catalogs musical systems, instruments, and scales from around the world. It supports Codex in generating regional themes, faction music, and non-Western audio profiles.

---

## 🌍 Regional Traditions

**Name:** *Smithsonian Folkways — Global Music Map*  
**Type:** Cultural Archive  
**URL:** https://folkways.si.edu/map-world  

**Use:** Catalog of real-world musical forms, instruments, and structure. Codex can use this to assign faction-specific audio logic or simulate regional identity.

---

**Name:** *Traditional Japanese Music – Nippon.com*  
**Type:** Cultural Overview  
**URL:** https://www.nippon.com/en/guide-to-japan/gu900121/  

**Use:** Describes pentatonic structure, shakuhachi, and percussive patterns. Codex can use this for serene, minimal, or disciplined faction styles.

---

**Name:** *Carnatic vs Hindustani – Classical Indian Systems*  
**Type:** Music Theory Overview  
**URL:** https://www.sruti.com/articles/general/carnatic-and-hindustani-music  

**Use:** Guides Codex in building complex raga-based procedural scores or multi-layered call/response forms.

---

## 🪕 Instrumentation Libraries

**Name:** *Iowa Musical Instrument Samples*  
**Type:** Free Instrument Sample Database  
**URL:** https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html  

**Use:** Audio recordings of real-world orchestral and ethnic instruments. Can be used by procedural systems to simulate factional orchestration.

---

**Name:** *Africa Sounds – BBC Sound Archive*  
**Type:** Regional Audio Archive  
**URL:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/sounds/play/p02s9zlp  

**Use:** Percussion-dominant audio for tribal and chaos-based groups. Can guide Codex in defining rhythmic intensity and energy type.
