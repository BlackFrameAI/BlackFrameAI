# Procedural Sound Design Manifest

This manifest provides resources for building procedural sound generation systems. It covers synthesis theory, waveform construction, modulation, and audio layering techniques for dynamic runtime sound.

---

## 🔊 Sound Synthesis & Modulation

**Name:** *Introduction to Sound Synthesis – MIT OpenCourseWare*  
**Type:** University Lecture Notes  
**URL:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/music-and-theater-arts/21m-370-digital-sound-design-fall-2005/lecture-notes/  

**Use:** Covers additive, subtractive, and FM synthesis techniques. Codex can use this to model sound sources like powerups, teleport effects, charging sounds, or entropy pulses from simple waveforms.

---

**Name:** *Designing Sound* (Andy Farnell)  
**Type:** Audio Programming Book  
**URL:** http://aspress.co.uk/ds/  

**Use:** Teaches procedural audio modeling of physical interactions (glass, gunshots, liquids). Useful for real-time generation of reactive game sounds without needing samples or pre-recorded clips.

---

**Name:** *Pure Data Tutorials – Sound Examples*  
**Type:** Practical Audio Engine Demos  
**URL:** https://puredata.info/downloads/tutorials  

**Use:** Offers real-world examples of procedural audio patches and modulation chains. Useful for Codex when designing looping ambient beds or reactive noise effects.

---

## 🌌 Ambient Layering & Spatial Effects

**Name:** *Designing Immersive Audio Spaces – GameSoundCon*  
**Type:** Industry Panel Summary  
**URL:** https://www.gamesoundcon.com/s/Immersive_Audio_GameSoundCon.pdf  

**Use:** Describes spatial layering of ambient zones, dynamic soundscapes, and player-reactive audio shifts. Codex can apply these techniques to procedural environments or biome-linked sound layers.

---

**Name:** *3D Sound and Environmental Acoustics – Dolby White Paper*  
**Type:** Technical Research Paper  
**URL:** https://professional.dolby.com/siteassets/pdfs/3d-audio-environment.pdf  

**Use:** Helps Codex define decay, occlusion, and motion-driven audio behaviors. Useful for room reverb, spatialized UI sounds, or chaos-origin pulse trails.
