# rumors_and_folklore.md

This document captures in-world **rumors**, **superstitions**, and **unverified lore** circulating among the factions and peoples of *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

These entries are intentionally unreliable → CODEX should treat them as **flavor** — not hard lore.

## Rumors and Folklore

---

### The Vanished Fleet

- A battlefleet of the Eternal Empire vanished after pursuing Veil forces into an uncharted system.
- Some say the Veil swallowed them.
- Others claim they still fight an endless crusade in a timeless void.

---

### The Singing Stone of Rhyax

- A black obelisk found on a remote mining world.
- Emits a haunting song heard only by the Faithful or the Damned — reports vary.
- Several expeditions to study it have disappeared.

---

### The Third Inquisitorial Seal

- Supposedly an artifact of immense power.
- Rumored to grant immunity to corruption — or to **force others to confess** through supernatural means.
- Its existence is denied by the Orders — which only fuels the rumors.

---

### Skitterkin Soulbinding

- Whispers claim certain Skitterkin are "soulbound" — eternally reincarnated in service to the Veil.
- No verifiable evidence exists — but some Imperial inquisitors believe it.

---

### The Weeping Planet

- A long-abandoned world where it **rains blood** under certain moons.
- Imperial records claim this is natural mineral runoff.
- Cultists claim it is a sign of the Veil’s hunger.

---

### The Faceless Faith

- A **secret sect** rumored to exist within the Empire itself.
- Preaches that **Faith and Corruption are one** — dual faces of a cosmic truth.
- No known members have ever been captured.

### Veil-Sight Phenomena

- Certain individuals are rumored to be born with **Veil-Sight** — a cursed gift allowing them to perceive flows of corruption invisible to others.
- Veil-Sight is said to drive most to madness, while a rare few harness it for tactical or prophetic advantage.
- No verified cases exist — or so the Inquisition claims.

---

### Deep Zone Anomalies

- In the galactic core and other “Deep Zones” of Veil corruption, bizarre spatial anomalies are reported:
    - Non-Euclidean structures
    - Temporal distortions
    - Psychic bleed phenomena
- Cults often seek pilgrimage to these Deep Zones.
- Few return.
