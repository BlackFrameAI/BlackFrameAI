# rules_for_veil.md

Guidelines to ensure consistency, IP safety, and tone alignment for *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

This document informs CODEX generation behavior for game content, lore, art direction, and narrative tone.

---

## Satire Guidelines and Risk Mitigation

- **Purpose**: To clarify the satirical themes and ensure they remain edgy but safe, avoiding cancellation or legal risks.

- **Satirical Themes**:
    - **Eternal Empire**: Embraces Christian crusade symbology and zealotry — gothic cathedrals, crosses, litanies, holy war imagery. Avoids directly naming Christianity.
    - **Crescent Veil**: Clearly emulates Muslim religious structure and laws, through fictional "Veil Law," crescent motifs, veiled figures, faux-Arabic elements — but avoids direct naming of Islam.
    - **Skitterkin**: Allegory for modern mass immigration — presented as xenos swarms serving the Crescent Veil — without naming or paralleling real-world groups.

- **Risk Mitigation**:
    - No real-world names, events, or cultural groups.
    - Satire must balance all factions.
    - Sci-fi framing: all factions/species are fictional.
    - Narrator: Zealous vox-filtered drill sergeant and other factional voices — satirical tone.
    - Disclaimer on main menu: "*Purge of the Crescent Veil* is an original work. Not affiliated with Games Workshop."

## Future Development Notes

- **Skitterkin and Crescent Veil**: Both factions are intended to eventually support **playable classes**, not only enemy roles. All faction/playable roles are modular and subject to expansion as the game evolves.
- **Unit Expansion**: Planned 20+ playable classes — depth and variety goal.

- **Neutral Factions and Races**: The game will include additional neutral races, rogue factions, and random enemies, beyond the three core factions. These may also be expanded into playable content in future versions.

## Visual and Audio Style Guide

- **Visuals**:
    - **Eternal Empire**: Aurorite Plate, oversized gothic weapons, gothic sci-fi backdrops, crusade banners.
    - **Crescent Veil**: Veiled cultists, crescent motifs, purple aesthetic, religious shrines.
    - **Skitterkin**: Xenos insectoid — distinct from existing IP.

- **Audio**:
    - Grimdark choirs, industrial ambience.
    - Narrators: Multiple personalities — player’s chosen Chapter or role may alter narrator voice:
        - Vox-filtered drill sergeant
        - Inquisitor tone
        - Librarian/scholar tone
        - Specialist voices tied to player’s faction/chapter.
    - Faux-language chants for cultists.

## Licensing and IP Avoidance Strategy

- **Commercial release planned** → IP-safe execution required.
- Factions, unit names, and gameplay mechanics must remain original.
- Core inspirations:
    - Starship Troopers
    - Warhammer 40k (tone and themes — with strict IP boundaries)
    - Fallout (visual influences)
    - Gundam (mech elements)
    - Historical Christian Crusades (symbolism, zealotry, aesthetic inspiration)
    - Gothic sci-fi
    - Religious crusade imagery (historical/fictional — public domain)
    - Roguelike and action games:
        - Hades
        - Brotato
        - Binding of Isaac
        - Path of Exile (passive trees and systems)
        - Vampire Survivors
        - Tiny Rogues
        - Others in similar genres (for gameplay pacing and system choices)
- No use of GW trademarks, settings, unit names, or distinctive design elements.
- Prior to public release, final IP review required.
