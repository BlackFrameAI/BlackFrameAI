# Audio Theory Manifest

This manifest provides Codex with foundational sources on music theory, tonal structure, harmony, and emotional influence through sound. It informs alignment-based musical systems, reactive ambience, and procedural audio behavior.

---

## 🎼 Music Theory & Structure

**Name:** *Music Theory for Musicians and Normal People* (Toby Rush)  
**Type:** Illustrated Educational Series  
**URL:** https://tobyrush.com/theorypages/  

**Use:** Core music theory — scales, modes, intervals, keys, and harmonic function. Used to define faction themes, emotional tone in environments, and musical motifs tied to player alignment or tension.

---

**Name:** *Open Music Theory*  
**Type:** Collaborative Online Textbook  
**URL:** https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/  

**Use:** In-depth breakdown of Western harmonic progression, meter, rhythm, and counterpoint. Codex can use this to generate complex musical layers and maintain stylistic consistency in ambient or dynamic scores.

---

## 🎹 Tonal Emotion Mapping

**Name:** *The Psychology of Music* (D. Deutsch, ed.)  
**Type:** Academic Reference Text  
**URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9780123814609/the-psychology-of-music  

**Use:** Cognitive and emotional impact of pitch, tempo, interval selection, and dissonance. Useful for mood shaping in interactive or procedural music systems (e.g., slower tempo in safe zones, high-pitched arpeggios during danger).

---

**Name:** *Film Scoring 101* — Berklee College of Music  
**Type:** Course Material / Lecture  
**URL:** https://online.berklee.edu/courses/film-scoring-101  

**Use:** How instrumentation, theme, and timing affect player perception. Codex can pull phrasing techniques for battle music vs exploration cues, or alignment-based tonal changes.

---

## 🔁 Procedural Audio & Harmony Control

**Name:** *Generative Music Techniques – Tero Parviainen*  
**Type:** Presentation/Research  
**URL:** https://teropa.info/blog/2016/07/28/generative-music.html  

**Use:** Concepts for chord-based generative music, live harmony changes, and rule-based audio layers. Useful for entropy-modulated harmony systems or chaos-reactive music synthesis.
