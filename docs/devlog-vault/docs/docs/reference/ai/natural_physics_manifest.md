# Natural Physics Reference Manifest

This manifest grounds Codex in environmental physics  simulating terrain deformation, wind behavior, natural structure motion, and landscape-driven reactions.

##  Wind Dynamics & Aerodynamic Force

**Name:** *Fluid Flow Over Terrain  NOAA Wind Resource Atlas*  
**Type:** Government Data Portal  
**URL:** https://www.nrel.gov/grid/wind-resource.html  

**Use:** Models wind drag over hills, buildings, or forests. Codex may simulate sandstorm flow, directional gusts, or exposed high-ground debuffs.

---

**Name:** *Aerodynamic Drag & Lift  NASA Glenn Research Center*  
**Type:** Aerospace Physics  
**URL:** https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/drageq.html  

**Use:** Simulates how natural objects move or resist wind. Codex may model leaf flutter, cape physics, or arrow drift in storms.
