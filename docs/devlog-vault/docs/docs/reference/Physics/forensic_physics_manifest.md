# Forensic Physics Reference Manifest

This manifest supports Codex in building accurate systems related to injury mechanics, trauma modeling, explosion propagation, and high-velocity material behavior. All references are grounded in real-world forensic science and combat wound dynamics.

---

##  Explosions & Shockwaves

**Name:** *FBI Bomb Data Center  Post-Blast Scene Analysis*  
**Type:** Government Forensics Guide  
**URL:** https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/post-blast-scene-analysis-guide.pdf  

**Use:** Defines fragmentation radius, blast pressure zones, heat vs concussive damage. Codex can use this to simulate explosion effects on environments or NPCs with physics-driven accuracy.

---

**Name:** *Explosive Effects and Consequences  U.S. Army Technical Manual TM 5-1300*  
**Type:** Military Engineering Manual  
**URL:** https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/ARMYCOE/COETM/tm_5-1300.pdf  

**Use:** Describes the impact of blast waves on structural and biological targets. Supports Codex when modeling cratering, body dismemberment, or armor failure.

---

##  Gunshot Wounds & Ballistics

**Name:** *Gunshot Wounds: Practical Aspects of Firearms, Ballistics, and Forensic Techniques*  
**Type:** Forensics Textbook  
**URL:** https://www.amazon.com/Gunshot-Wounds-Practical-Ballistics-Forensic/dp/0367331581  

**Use:** Covers penetration behavior, tissue cavitation, temporary wound channels. Codex may use this for real-time wound simulation, armor piercing logic, or projectile-bone interaction.

---

**Name:** *NIJ Ballistics Standards*  
**Type:** Government Standard  
**URL:** https://www.nij.ojp.gov/topics/articles/ballistic-resistance-body-armor-nij-standard-010106  

**Use:** Reference for armor resistance levels and kinetic thresholds. Used for procedural combat simulations or weapon testing overlays.

##  Fracture Mechanics & Trauma

**Name:** *Mechanics of Bone Fracture  NIH PubMed Central*  
**Type:** Research Article  
**URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9480724/  

**Use:** Describes bone shatter dynamics under different force vectors. Codex may simulate high-impact ragdoll collapse or bone integrity loss from crush or fall trauma.

---

**Name:** *Blunt Force Trauma Patterns  Forensic Pathology Atlas*  
**Type:** Medical Guide  
**URL:** https://forensicmd.files.wordpress.com/2013/01/blunt-trauma.pdf  

**Use:** Classifies bruising, laceration, and skull fracture types. Codex can assign damage regions or simulate delayed death animations based on impact trajectory.
