# Chemistry Reaction Manifest

Codex uses these references to simulate real-world reactive systems — combustion, acid/base behavior, volatility thresholds, and ignition properties. These sources guide particle effects, procedural hazard generation, and alchemical logic.

---

## 🔥 Combustion & Ignition Reactions

**Name:** NIST Chemistry WebBook – Thermochemical Data\
**Type:** US Gov Database\
**URL:** https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/

Use: Reaction heat, ignition points, gas production, flame color. Basis for fire, explosion, and reactive trap mechanics.

---

## ⚗️ Acid/Base Volatility

**Name:** CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics (Acid-Base Constants)\
**Type:** Textbook Reference Summary\
**URL:** https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/

Use: pKa/pKb values, corrosive thresholds, acid damage modeling. Use for liquid effect visuals, chemical bombs, decomposition.

---

## 💣 Explosive Reactants & Triggers

**Name:** Department of Homeland Security Explosives Chemistry Report\
**Type:** Government Research Archive\
**URL:** https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/publications/Explosives_Chemistry_Report.pdf

Use: Volatility chains, binary explosive behavior, detonation curves. Useful for bomb crafting systems or unstable terrain effects.
