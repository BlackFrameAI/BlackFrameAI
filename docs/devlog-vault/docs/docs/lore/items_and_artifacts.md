# items_and_artifacts.md

This document defines notable artifacts, relics, and legendary items within the world of *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

These items serve as narrative anchors, quest drivers, or gameplay-enhancing elements.

---

## Core Principles

- Artifacts are rare and meaningful — NOT generic loot.
- Many artifacts are **Veil-corrupted**, **Faith-sanctified**, or of **unknown origin**.
- Their use often comes with:
    - Faction standing impacts
    - Alignment shifts
    - World state changes
    - Narrative consequences

---

## Sample Notable Artifacts

---

### The Tome of Entropic Faith

- A heretical codex — believed to predate the current Veil Wars.
- Contains rites that blend Faith and Veil principles — highly unstable.
- Possession is punishable by death in most Imperial sectors.

---

### The Black Halo

- An ancient auramantic relic — emits an anti-faith field.
- Disrupts Faith-based powers within a radius.
- Used by radical Crescent Veil cult leaders.

---

### The Scepter of Silent Judgement

- An Inquisitorial artifact.
- Focuses Faith energy into lethal precision strikes.
- Known to amplify user zealotry to dangerous extremes.

---

### Shard of the First Corruption

- Believed to be a fragment of the first material touched by the Veil.
- Emits an aura of passive corruption — deforms nearby matter.
- Highly sought after by both cultists and Inquisitors (for study/destruction).

---

### Sanctified Engines (Mech Relics)

- Precursor machines partially cleansed by Faith rites.
- Still exhibit unpredictable behavior — prone to "awakening" during battle.
- Pilots undergo rigorous Faith conditioning to operate them.

---

## Notes for CODEX

- Artifact generation should prioritize narrative significance over raw stat boosts.
- Most artifacts should be:
    - Unique
    - Named
    - Tied to faction lore or major events
- Artifact acquisition may impact faction relations, alignment, or world state.
- Artifacts should support **mechanical risk/reward design** — using one should always carry consequences.
