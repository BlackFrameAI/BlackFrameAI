# powers_and_rites.md

This document defines the rules, constraints, and world logic behind supernatural powers, rites, and esoteric abilities in *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

All content here is narrative-canonical and informs CODEX-driven scaffolding for powers, ability systems, and faction abilities.

---

## Core Principles

- Supernatural powers in this universe stem from **interaction with the Veil** and **Faith**.
- Powers manifest through:
    - Rites
    - Esoteric symbols
    - Blood rituals
    - Chanting and invocations
    - Embedded artifacts (runed weapons, corrupted or sanctified technology)
- There is **no innate “magic”** as in fantasy settings → all powers are emergent from interaction with the Veil or with Faith counterforces.
- Faith-based miracles are also treated as "powers" → there is no mechanical difference from corrupted abilities — both follow system rules.
- The war between these forces is one of **mirrored zealotry** — both claim righteousness, both inflict devastation.

---

## General Rules of Power

- The Veil **corrupts and enables** → those who learn its principles can harness it at a cost.
- Faith-based powers operate as **oppositional force** — but are equally capable of violence, subjugation, and world-alteration.
- Both Faith and Veil powers can be used by player characters, depending on alignment and Chapter.
- Powers are meant to reflect the **dangers of religious zealotry** — both forces reshape the world to their ideals.

---

## Laws of Power Use

- **Energy Source:** All powers draw from either:
    - **Corruption** (Veil resonance)
    - **Faith** (Eternal Light resonance)
    - Or in rare cases → neutral ancient forces (Precursor tech, Wild Rites).
- **Channeling:** Powers require channeling through ritual, gesture, or technological interface.
- **Cost:** Every power has a cost:
    - Resource drain (HP, stamina, corruption buildup, Faith depletion, etc.)
    - Physical toll on the user
    - Social or faction consequences
- **Instability:** Veil-derived powers become unstable at extreme levels of Corruption → can lead to mutation, loss of control, or factional exile.
- **Zealous Overreach:** Faith-based powers can trigger **fanatic states** → self-harming fervor, collateral damage, or loss of control.

---

## Forbidden Powers

- Certain rites and powers are classified as **forbidden** by both factions.
- Use of forbidden rites may trigger:
    - Imperial bounty events
    - Faction betrayal mechanics
    - Increased Veil corruption or uncontrolled Faith fanaticism in the region

---

## Balance of Powers

- There is NO pure "good/evil" power split.
- Both Faith and Veil-aligned abilities encompass:
    - Offensive powers
    - Defensive powers
    - Utility powers
    - Corruptive/cleansing world-altering effects
- Class and Chapter design will determine access to these abilities — but no archetype is locked to "good" or "evil."

---

## Examples of Powers Across All Alignments

The following are EXAMPLES of powers available to characters and factions in *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

These lists are NOT exhaustive and DO NOT imply moral distinctions between Faith and Veil powers.

Both sides employ abilities of violence, defense, manipulation, and subjugation.

---

**Faith-Aligned Powers**

- Blazing smite
- Purging flame
- Forced conversion
- Battlefield blessings
- Mass executions (sanctioned)
- Exorcistic detonations
- Divine firestorm
- Zeal-driven berserker states
- Sanctified corruption of terrain (Faith zones)

---

**Veil-Aligned Powers**

- Entropic blasts
- Blood-leech rituals
- Corruption waves
- Voidfire barrages
- Subversion chants
- Shadow summons
- Mindbreaking sigils
- Viral memetic curses
- Corruption of terrain (Veil zones)

---

**Neutral/Unaligned/Ancient Powers**

- Precursor tech weaponry
- Wild rites of unknown origin
- Psionic disruptions
- Forbidden hybrid abilities (cross-contamination)

---

**Corruption and Cleansing Symmetry**

- Veil corruption visibly alters environments, entities, and artifacts — spreading biological, psychic, and memetic decay.
- Faith cleansing is not neutral — it imposes zealous purification that ALSO transforms environments and entities — creating "sanctified corruption" in its own image.
- Both forces **change the world** — neither restores an original "pure" state.

---

## Notes for CODEX

- No "generic magic" → powers must follow these faction-aligned systems.
- All abilities must have **logical costs and risks**.
- Powers should reflect the mirrored fanaticism of both Faith and Veil forces.
- Systems should support future expansion of:
    - Faction-specific rites
    - Forbidden powers
    - Alignment-reactive abilities
    - Artifact-enhanced powers
