# factions.md

This document defines the core factions of *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

All content here is gameplay-facing, narrative-canonical, and intended to guide CODEX-driven systems, encounters, and progression.

---

## Factions Overview

---

### Eternal Empire

**Theme:** Crusading zealotry, gothic sci-fi imperialism, religious militarism.

**Lore Summary:**  
The Eternal Empire spans countless worlds, unified by faith in the Light Eternal. It wages endless crusades to purge the corrupting influence of the Crescent Veil. Its warriors bear Aurorite Plate, fight with sacred arms, and march beneath banners of ancient faith. Its bureaucracy is vast, its inquisitors ruthless, its devotion absolute.

**Structure:**  
- Hierarchical orders → player chooses **Chapter** (formerly Creed).
- Chapters influence Faith/Corruption alignment and visual/auditory narrative tone.
- Faction-driven progression system tied to reputation and deeds.

**Visual Notes:**  
- Crosses, gothic cathedrals, crusader imagery.
- Vox-filtered narrator (varies by Chapter).

---

### Crescent Veil

**Theme:** Cultic devotion, religious fundamentalism, alien corruption.

**Lore Summary:**  
The Crescent Veil is not a faction — it is a metaphysical presence, worshipped by veiled cultists who impose theocratic rule under "Veil Law." Its influence spreads insidiously through worlds. The cult draws power from the Veil itself, fueling rites, glyphs, and dark technologies.

**Structure:**  
- Nominal leader: a female figure known as the "Veil Mother" — a heretical twist on patriarchal religious orthodoxy.  
- Cult cells operate semi-independently but revere the Veil Mother’s edicts and symbolic authority.  
- Rumored to ride a massive bioengineered xeno-beast ("Great Swine" or similar — WIP).
- Player factions within Crescent Veil (planned for future expansions).
- Player alignment (Faith/Corruption) influences interactions with Veil forces.

**Visual Notes:**  
- Crescent motifs, purple/black aesthetic.
- Faux-Arabic calligraphy and architecture.
- Veiled figures.

---

### Skitterkin

**Theme:** Xenos swarm, cultural allegory, societal disruption.

**Lore Summary:**  
The Skitterkin are a hyper-adaptive species bound in servitude to the Crescent Veil — though the depth of their loyalty varies. Once native to forgotten worlds, they now infest Veil-controlled space. Rumors persist of a future Skitterkin rebellion. Some leaders seek freedom; others deepen their ties to Veil cults.

**Structure:**  
- Clan-based hierarchy.
- Faction status → enemies at start → planned playable classes.
- Visual corruption based on Faith/Corruption influence.

**Visual Notes:**  
- Insectoid designs — unique silhouettes.
- Biotech enhancements.
- Swarm behavior in combat.

---

### Future Narrative Hooks: Skitterkin Rebellion

- While Skitterkin owe an unbreakable life-debt to the Veil, there are **rumors of dissent**.
- Whispers speak of certain Skitterkin leaders questioning their servitude.
- The emergence of **rogue Skitterkin factions** is a potential future narrative arc.
- These factions could provide:
    - Player faction choice opportunities
    - New enemy types
    - Playable Skitterkin paths in future expansions

---

### Neutral & Rogue Factions (Planned)

**Theme:** Independent actors, wildcard elements.

**Lore Summary:**  
Beyond the three core factions, the galaxy hosts neutral planets, rogue bands, and emergent species. These include:

- Mercenary groups
- Technocratic enclaves
- Isolated survivor colonies
- Precursor AI remnants
- Unaligned xenos species

These groups will feature in storylines, encounters, and faction reputation systems.

## Notes for CODEX

- Faction system must remain **modular** and support expansion.
- Faction names, structures, and relationships may evolve as game design progresses.
- Narratives and mechanics tied to Faith/Corruption must integrate with faction logic.
- Player agency (joining, betraying, allying) is an intended future feature — scaffold systems with this flexibility.
