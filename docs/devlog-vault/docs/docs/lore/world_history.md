# world_history.md

This document defines the key historical events, timelines, and major lore arcs of *Purge of the Crescent Veil*.

All content here is narrative-canonical and informs CODEX-driven worldbuilding, faction development, and story branching.

---

## The Nature of the Veil

- The Veil is **not a dimension**.
- It is the **metaphysical SOURCE of corruption** — an ever-present force that manifests through psychic influence, matter corruption, and societal rot.
- It opposes the counterforce of **Faith**, embodied by the Eternal Empire’s crusades.
- The Veil’s presence informs:
    - Veil cult **runes**, **sigils**, and **rites**.
    - Theocratic "Veil Law" — modeled on strict religious codes.
    - Biological and technological corruption of both matter and mind.
- The **origins of the Veil** are ancient and disputed — some legends trace its shadow back to **ancient Terran religious wars**.

---

## The Purge

- The "Purge" refers to the Eternal Empire’s **millennia-spanning crusade** against Veil corruption.
- The war began in humanity’s distant past — seeded in ancient conflicts and faith wars on Old Terra.
- Across the ages, it has mutated into an endless ideological and physical war of extermination.
- Today, it spans countless systems:
    - **Purge Zones** → regions under quarantine or crusader occupation.
    - **Corruption Fronts** → active warzones.
    - **Veil Holds** → cult-controlled worlds.

---

## Major Historical Arcs

---

### Ancient Origins of the Veil

- **Earliest recorded references** date back to pre-spaceflight human history.
- Ancient heresies, plagues, and "shadow faiths" are believed by some scholars to have been early manifestations of the Veil’s influence.
- The first Imperial dogmas codified the Veil as **the Great Corruption** — the antithesis of the Eternal Light.

---

### Rise of the Skitterkin

- The Skitterkin’s origins are **unknown** — ancient xenos species.
- Prior to Veil contact, they existed as **nomadic mercenary clans** — technologically advanced raiders.
- The Skitterkin owe the Veil an **unbreakable life-debt** — the terms of which are lost to history.
- They now serve as shock troops and enforcers of the Crescent Veil.
    - Loyalty is culturally absolute.
    - Defection is a capital offense among their kind.
    - Potential **future rogue Skitterkin factions** are seeded for narrative expansion.

---

### The Inquisitorial Orders

- The **Inquisitorial Orders** trace their roots to ancient Terran religious inquisitions.
- Over millennia, they have evolved into a vast, clandestine intelligence and purge force within the Eternal Empire.
- Their structure, rites, and authority mirror early inquisitorial traditions, adapted to galactic scale.

---

### The Concordat Collapse

- At some point in the **distant past**, an interstellar diplomatic accord — the "Concordat of Enlightened Systems" — attempted to unify factions against the Veil.
- The Concordat ultimately collapsed under Veil subversion.
- Today, its legacy is fragmented:
    - Some Neutral/Rogue factions trace origins to Concordat member worlds.
    - Others represent **indigenous populations** of contested planets — caught between Empire and Veil crusades.

---

### The Endless Crusade (Current Era)

- The Purge is ongoing, adaptive, and without foreseeable end.
- The Veil itself evolves:
    - New **rites**, **heresies**, and **corrupted technologies** emerge constantly.
    - Faction lines are fluid — entire worlds shift allegiance with corruption waves.
- Ancient relics and precursor technologies are rediscovered — many tainted by the Veil.
- Neutral systems struggle to survive amid ceaseless war.

---

## Rumored Pre-Veil History

- Fragmentary records suggest older civilizations — predating current human expansion — may have encountered similar corruptive phenomena.
- Legends tell of ancient Veil-linked travelers who "vanished into other realms" — whether metaphor, myth, or suppressed truth is unknown.
- These tales are used heavily in cult propaganda but remain classified as speculative.

---

### Rumored Precursor Species

- Some Imperial scholars speak of a possible **precursor civilization** predating both the Eternal Empire and the known xenos empires.
- Fragmentary artifacts, star charts, and deep void structures hint at this lost race.
- Some cults claim these precursors were **Veil-born**, or perhaps the first beings to be consumed by the Veil.
- Others argue they were builders of the first **Faith Engines**, long before the rise of the Eternal Empire.
- No consensus exists — the topic remains highly classified within the Orders.

## Notes for CODEX

- The Veil is the **source of corruption** — it is not a place or traversable dimension.
- Death is permanent — no resurrection mechanics.
- Historical arcs should inform faction relations, story branching, and emergent world state mechanics.
- Timeline is modular — future expansions may add new historical events, factions, or heresies.
