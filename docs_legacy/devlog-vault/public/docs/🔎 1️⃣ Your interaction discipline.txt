🔎 Interaction Discipline Overview (Public Summary)
This note outlines the general habits used to keep AI collaboration reliable without revealing private prompts or enforcement scripts.

✅ Guiding Principles
- Treat the model as a probabilistic assistant whose outputs require verification.
- Separate factual results from the reasoning steps the AI provides.
- Use recurring check-ins to confirm shared understanding before proceeding.

🧠 Helpful Behaviors
- Ask the AI to restate requirements to surface misunderstandings early.
- Document decisions outside the conversation so state does not drift.
- Encourage honest error reporting by framing mistakes as part of the process.

🚧 Common Pitfalls for New Operators
- Accepting fluent language as proof of correctness.
- Skipping constraint reminders, which lets the AI extrapolate beyond scope.
- Reacting emotionally to failures instead of refining the request.

📌 Takeaway
Success comes from disciplined supervision and transparent logging, not from assuming the AI is always right on the first try.
