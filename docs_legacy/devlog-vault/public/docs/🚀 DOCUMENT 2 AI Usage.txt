🚀 AI Usage Protocol (Sanitized Summary)

This public summary captures the intent of the internal AI usage guidelines while removing sensitive operational steps.

Key Principles
- Treat AI systems as logic amplifiers directed by trained operators.
- Maintain human arbitration, auditing, and safety oversight at every stage.
- Favor transparent reasoning, traceable task decomposition, and permissioned automation.

High-Level Workflow Overview
1. Humans define goals and guardrails.
2. A planning model drafts scaffolding proposals for review.
3. A coding model executes approved, atomic tasks under supervision.
4. Feedback loops confirm correctness before integration.
5. Humans perform the final merge and validation.

Governance Rules
- Unknowns must trigger clarification instead of guesses.
- Each output must cite its reasoning path or defer to a human when uncertain.
- Automated recovery is limited to pre-authorized scenarios; otherwise escalation is required.

Audit & Error Handling
- Every stage includes a structured self-audit to surface risks early.
- Escalation paths exist for ambiguous instructions, emission errors, or constraint drift.
- Fail-safe behavior prioritizes halting and alerting over speculative fixes.
