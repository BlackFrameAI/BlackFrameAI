## Artifact Design Pillars

- Treat relics as narrative keystones rather than routine loot drops.
- Align each item with sanctified, shroud-tainted, or ambiguous origins to drive player choice.
- Expect each discovery to influence faction standing, alignment drift, or world-state conditions.

---

## Representative Categories

- **Forbidden Tomes:** Hybrid doctrines that destabilize the barrier between holy and profane energies.
- **Null Halo Devices:** Relics engineered to suppress or distort faith-powered abilities in a localized field.
- **Judgement Implements:** Sanctified weapons that magnify zeal at the risk of fanaticism.
- **Corruption Shards:** Residue from the first outbreaks of otherworldly influence; hazardous but invaluable for research.
- **Awakened Engines:** Semi-sentient war machines requiring rigorous conditioning before deployment.

---

## Implementation Notes for CODEX

- Prioritize bespoke naming and narrative hooks when generating new artifacts.
- Couple acquisition events with meaningful consequences or branching storylines.
- Balance rewards with mechanical risk so that every relic feels momentous and double-edged.
