# factions.md (Sanitized Overview)

This public brief summarizes the faction landscape without exposing restricted codenames or ritual markers.

---

## Major Power Blocs

### Martial Dominion
- Galaxy-spanning theocracy committed to eradicating corruption.
- Structured into orders that guide faith-versus-corruption alignment systems.
- Audio/visual identity: cathedral-scale architecture, liturgical comms, crusader motifs.

### Shrouded Hierophants
- Esoteric cult devoted to an unseen cosmic presence.
- Operate through semi-autonomous cells directed by an oracular matriarch (designation classified).
- Iconography blends lunar symbolism, calligraphic sigils, and veiled silhouettes.

### Bound Swarm
- Hyper-adaptive xeno society press-ganged into cult servitude.
- Organized by clans seeking either liberation or deeper allegiance.
- Provides future hooks for rebellion storylines and playable archetypes.

### Independent Actors
- Mercenaries, technocrats, survivor enclaves, precursor remnants, and unaffiliated species.
- Serve as neutral or wildcard forces within faction reputation systems.
