# Gameplay Systems (Public Overview)

The following summary highlights the major gameplay layers in *Purge of the Crescent Veil* while omitting sensitive implementation data.

## Gameplay Pillars

- **Alignment & Factions** — Faith and Corruption influences unlock powers, affect NPC behavior, and reshape encounters.
- **Procedural Combat Stages** — Runs progress through handcrafted beats that are remixed with procedural modifiers to maintain replayability.
- **Hub & Meta Progression** — Between missions, players invest in chapter standing, vendors, and upgrades that persist across sessions.
- **Space Operations** — Optional ship combat missions complement ground gameplay and share resources with the core progression loop.

## System Highlights

| System | Summary |

| Environmental Play | Corruption level, hazards, and destructible props reinforce the narrative tone of each stage. |
| Audio & Atmosphere | Procedurally mixed music and effects that react to combat intensity and exploration pacing. |
