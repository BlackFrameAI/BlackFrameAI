# Game Design (Public Overview)

This document summarizes the player-facing vision for *Purge of the Crescent Veil* without exposing internal implementation notes.

## Core Experience Goals

- Deliver a 2D action RPG foundation with occasional cinematic 3D moments for major encounters.
- Emphasize faction-driven storytelling where choices influence the world state and character progression.
- Support replayability through modular chapters, procedural encounters, and optional mission types.

## Factions & Alignment

- Major factions include the Eternal Empire, Crescent Veil cults, Skitterkin, and various independent groups.
- Player reputation shifts according to story decisions, combat outcomes, and alliance choices.
- Alignment along Faith and Corruption axes unlocks bespoke powers, gear, and narrative branches.

## Characters & Progression

- Chapters act as sub-factions that grant themed abilities, passives, and narrative arcs.
- Progression blends character level, chapter standing, and hub upgrades to pace new tools and challenges.
- Powers and rites replace legacy glyph mechanics, streamlining build customization for future updates.

## Combat Pillars

- Responsive 8-way movement with defensive maneuvers and distinct weapon slots.
- Enemies adapt to faction relationships and alignment levels to keep encounters dynamic.
- Boss battles unfold in multi-phase waves that reflect story developments and player decisions.

## Complementary Systems

- Procedural sprite technology supplies a cohesive visual style without depending on pre-rendered textures.
- Optional space-combat scenarios extend the campaign with ship-based progression that ties back into the hub.
- UI layers are rebuilt around data-driven widgets to maintain consistency across gameplay, menus, and overlays.

## Public Roadmap Highlights

- Expanded chapter questlines with unique rewards and branching outcomes.
- Refinement of hub activities, including vendors, crafting, and challenge terminals.
- Additional accessibility settings for input, readability, and audio balancing.
