# Purge of the Crescent Veil — Creative Summary

This public brief highlights the tone, inspirations, and goals that guide Purge of the Crescent Veil. It omits internal production details and focuses on the narrative and gameplay identity.

---

## Game Identity

- Genre blend of grimdark roguelike and action shooter.
- Built around combat, customization, faction interaction, and long-term progression.
- Delivers pointed satire of zealotry, imperialism, and faction hypocrisy.

---

## Influences

- Gothic science fiction and apocalyptic religious imagery.
- Classic and modern roguelike inspirations such as Hades, Brotato, The Binding of Isaac, Path of Exile, Vampire Survivors, and Tiny Rogues.
- Broader genre touchstones are treated as thematic references while respecting intellectual property boundaries.

---

## Factions and Tone

- No faction is purely heroic; both Faith and Veil forces are dangerous and morally compromised.
- The conflict spans millennia, with intentionally ambiguous origins.
- Magic and technology intertwine within combat-centric builds rather than isolated archetypes.
- Alignment choices influence both progression systems and narrative branches.

---

## Gameplay Focus

- Core loop emphasizes combat, glyph and rite experimentation, faction reputation, exploration, and escalation events.
- Systems support class and chapter identities, Faith/Corruption alignment, hub-based crafting, passive trees, procedural encounters, and boss battles.

---

## Visual and UX Direction

- Interface elements favor diegetic presentation informed by gothic sci-fi aesthetics.
- UI and world state should visibly react to the player's alignment and faction commitments.
- Persistent choices drive meaningful consequences that echo through subsequent runs.
