# Symbology Manifest (Public Summary)

This summary captures the symbolism research guiding Codex while removing esoteric concordances, trademarked icon sets, and confidential design briefs.

---

## Cultural Sign Systems
- **Source Category:** Public anthropology primers and semiotics textbooks.
- **Shared Data:** Gesture meanings, color hierarchies, and emblem archetypes safe for cross-cultural usage.
- **Withheld Data:** Community-guarded rites, restricted initiation symbols, and clan registries.

---

## Tactical & Warning Symbols
- **Source Category:** Open safety standards, humanitarian signage guidelines, and declassified field manuals.
- **Shared Data:** Shape grammars, contrast requirements, and modular layout strategies.
- **Withheld Data:** Classified mission overlays, encryption keys, and live operations notes.

---

## Arcane & Fictional Glyphs
- **Source Category:** Public fantasy literature analyses, game postmortems, and creative commons glyph libraries.
- **Shared Data:** Construction logic, layering patterns, and resonance themes adaptable to new IP.
- **Withheld Data:** Proprietary runic generators, unreleased concept packs, and licensing agreements.

---

## Usage Guidance
- Share this manifest with teams crafting UI, signage, or magical systems for public builds.
- Coordinate with legal and cultural advisors before introducing symbols that may overlap with protected marks or living traditions.
