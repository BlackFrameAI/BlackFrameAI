# Planetary Biosystems Manifest — Physics Overview

This summary captures the publicly shareable domains that inform Codex planetary simulations. Proprietary formulas, raw sensor archives, and partner identifiers remain secured offline.

---

## Planetary Crust & Mineral Dispersion
- **Source Category:** Government mineral survey digests and geological atlases cleared for public release.
- **Shared Data:** Ore frequency trends, biome composition bands, and terrain clustering heuristics.
- **Withheld Data:** High-resolution survey grids, weighting tables, and concession metadata.

---

## Climate Formation & Weather Patterns
- **Source Category:** Open climate modeling briefings and historical reanalysis summaries.
- **Shared Data:** Jet stream behavior, slope-driven rainfall tendencies, and seasonal transition cues.
- **Withheld Data:** Solver constants, ensemble calibration notes, and restricted forecast products.

---

## Planetary Chemistry & Atmospheres
- **Source Category:** Public astrobiology primers and planetary chemistry literature surveys.
- **Shared Data:** Atmospheric mixing limits, habitability heuristics, and exotic reaction examples.
- **Withheld Data:** Laboratory experiment logs, instrument specifications, and proprietary reaction datasets.
