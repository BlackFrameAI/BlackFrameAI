# Materials Reference Manifest — Physics Summary

The following overview retains only the public-safe material science descriptors required for simulation tuning. Proprietary specifications and vendor identifiers are excluded.

---

## Aerospace & Extreme Environment Materials
- **Source Category:** Aggregated, export-cleared performance digests for high-heat and high-velocity applications.
- **Shared Data:** Thermal thresholds, abrasion resistance tiers, and generalized structural fatigue curves.
- **Withheld Data:** Manufacturer formulas, classified fabrication steps, and restricted compliance reports.

---

## Protective Systems & Impact Response
- **Source Category:** Public ballistic and safety equipment standards that describe general protection philosophies.
- **Shared Data:** Qualitative impact ratings, deformation envelopes, and layering concepts.
- **Withheld Data:** Certification matrices, supplier codes, and instrumented trial footage.

---

## Material Failure & Trauma Modeling
- **Source Category:** Open-access medical training guides and engineering failure summaries curated for instructional use.
- **Shared Data:** Macroscopic fracture modes, surface response descriptors, and safety protocol cues.
- **Withheld Data:** Explicit trauma photography, experimental rig schematics, and sensitive investigative notes.

---

## Usage Guidance
- Use these descriptors when adjusting physics-driven VFX or interaction tuning for public builds.
- Consult the proprietary archive for exact coefficients only when operating under authorized confidentiality agreements.
