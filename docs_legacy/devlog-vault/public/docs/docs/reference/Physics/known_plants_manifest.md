# Known Plants Manifest — Public Summary

Sensitive chemical and ethnobotanical data remain secured offline. This public document captures only the gameplay-safe descriptors necessary for biome generation and crafting flavor.

---

## Botanical Identification Guidance
- **Source Category:** Public horticultural surveys and extension service summaries collected from open government repositories.
- **Available Data:** Regional presence bands, plant morphology groupings, climate tolerances, and resilience ratings.
- **Withheld Material:** Full taxonomic keys, geospatial distribution layers, and protected species dossiers.

## Potent and Hazardous Flora Overview
- **Source Category:** Public-safety advisories and educational toxicology briefs.
- **Available Data:** Potency tiers, broad symptom families, and generalized handling recommendations.
- **Withheld Material:** Precise compound names, dosage tables, and lab preparation instructions.

## Structural and Geometric Traits

- **Available Data:** Abstract shape families, parametric ranges for branching density, and environmental trigger examples.
- **Withheld Material:** Institution identifiers, proprietary simulation coefficients, and field imagery.
