Why this day matters

Day 10 is the inflection where Codex stops being an OpenGL-only author. The vault shows the renderer splitting into a vendor-agnostic interface while the same sprint funnels faction telemetry from altars, vendors, and satellites into every combat branch. Rendering, narrative, and systems design now share the same data spine.

Setup

The morning brief came with two contradictions: StageDetailOverlay could dramatize faction control, yet it still read stale hub data, and the Vulkan experiment from Phase 9.8 had nowhere safe to plug in. The engine log lays out the prerequisites—pass the live world state into StageManager before the intro plays, teach NoticeOverlay how to display altar results, and clear the MSVC warnings that were waiting to trip the Windows toolchain.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17

Gameplay had its own reckoning. Without a faction field on stage definitions the spawn tables never shifted, vendors sold the same stock every run, and altars couldn’t actually bend alignment.Game Devlog · 2025-06-17 The same entry spells out the fix list: randomize vendor inventory with alignment weighting, let PassivesSystem read JSON bonuses, and wire StageManager to the faction-aware pools before we even think about exporting telemetry.Game Devlog · 2025-06-17

Only after those foundations were locked did we greenlight the renderer split. The backend interface needed a clean seam, and the Vulkan path would only fly if the engine could survive the handle shuffle without dropping hub state.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17

Breakthroughs

The headline win is that seam. Codex carved RenderBackend out of RenderSystem, shipped an OpenGLBackend as the default, and stood up a Vulkan2Backend with device and swapchain scaffolding. A new --vulkan flag lets us flip the pipeline at runtime while the build scripts pull in the Vulkan SDK, and RenderSystem now hangs onto the backend through a std::unique_ptr so ownership is explicit.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17

In parallel, faction data finally flows coherently. StageManager forwards the active world state into StageDetailOverlay intros, NoticeOverlay announces altar results in real time, and the hub recolors itself based on the same alignment payload.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17 Game-side, the new faction field seeds enemy pools, VendorInventory shuffles its list each run with alignment weighting, and StarMap plus SpaceManager register the sector control that those encounters decide.Game Devlog · 2025-06-17

The sprint didn’t stop at presentation. SatelliteTelemetrySystem now exports optional live tracking data into the engine, QuantumSimulator feeds probabilistic twists into CombatSimulator, and the AdaptiveTrainer and OpposedForceTrainer duo curate escalating scenarios. Gameplay closes the loop by compiling those modules and letting CombatSimulator ingest telemetry-backed or seeded battles on demand.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17Game Devlog · 2025-06-17

Failures & Fixes

Splitting the renderer exposed the legacy assumption that RenderSystem owned the GLFW window. The first Vulkan boot left the handle dangling, so Codex moved ownership entirely into the backend and relegated RenderSystem to lifecycle delegation.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17

Cross-platform warnings were next. MSVC balked at implicit casts coming from stb_easy_font_print, forcing a round of <cstdint> includes and explicit conversions before the Windows toolchain would accept the build.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17

On the gameplay side, the lack of faction metadata meant StarMap and SpaceManager kept reporting neutral space even after altar decisions. Giving stage definitions a faction field and letting StageManager expand spawn types fixed the discrepancy, aligning the world map with what players actually fought.Game Devlog · 2025-06-17

Next Steps

Vulkan2Backend still carries a “implementation continues” tag, so tomorrow’s mandate is command-buffer submission, swapchain resize handling, and parity tests against the OpenGL path.Engine Devlog · 2025-06-17 Narrative systems also queued their march: DialogueTree branches and ChapterManager quests now exist, but they need telemetry-driven triggers from NarrativeEventSystem to pay off those betrayal hooks.Game Devlog · 2025-06-17 With telemetry streaming and the backend seam cut, Codex finally gets to chase visual parity without starving the story engine.
